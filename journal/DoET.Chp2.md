# Design of Everyday Things - Chapter 2 Response

## Execution and Evaluation
In **this chapter**, we distinguish the process humans go through to solve the problems in our life. This involves switching between *execution* and *evaluation*. When a problem comes up, we think of a solution, then execute that solution. Once we receive the result, we evaluate said result. If we evaluate we didn't get the result we want, we think of another solution, and the process repeats. This operates on both a conscious level when we're learning new things or solving difficult problems, and a subconscious level when we already understand the actions we're doing and the results we receive.
