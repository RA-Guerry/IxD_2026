# Design of Everyday Things - Chapter 4 Response
## Constraints
Constraints, despite what they sound like, are actually tools which people can use to tell what an object is supposed to do, or how to put together parts into a greater whole. By constraining the amount of ways people can interact with an object or have two objects interact with each other, the user will need to do much less guessing what they are supposed to do with something, or may even determine one obvious solution to a given problem.
