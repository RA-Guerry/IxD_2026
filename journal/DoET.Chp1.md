# Design of Everyday Things - Chapter 1 Response

## Discoverability and Understanding
From **Page 3** onwards, Don Norman goes to great lengths to emphasize the use of *discoverability* and *understanding* in discerning the use of any individual item. Discoverability means one's ability to figure out what an item can do, and how to do those things, while Understanding is a greater comprehension of how to work an item. It reminded me of an electric kettle I own, so I am going to explain things using those. 
![No-button Electric Kettle](https://www.republicoftea.com/cdn/shop/files/V20437_2.jpg?v=1763653201)
Most people would probably observe this as a simple electric kettle. It is just like a kettle I used to own. You put the water in, plug it in, and it will heat up your water. One needs no discoverability to figure out how to use this electric kettle, as most people in this day and age likely already understand this is a container you put water into, and how to plug something in. Depending on the type of kettle, one might lack some understandings which will be gained later, such as "only touch the rest of the kettle when it's done heating water, it is hot" or "there is a button on the top to open the lid", but both are not essential to the core functions of the kettle nor hard to figure out. 

![Multi-button Electric Kettle](https://www.allrecipes.com/thmb/VYjx3dw3hvvJfqoToL2AE3VABIQ=/1500x0/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc()/AllRecipes-COSORIElectricGooseneck-JenniferCausey-033-8233adadce27411890b16e906c7ceeb9.jpeg)
This electric kettle is slightly different. You may notice a large array of buttons at the base. I also now own a kettle similar to this one, though with buttons on the handle. Mine is a special kettle that is good for making tea. On it are 6 options, "delicate", "green", "white", "oolong", "French press", and "boil". Below that are two buttons, "start" and "keep warm". Much like Norman's example of the couple with the complicated washing machine, I have done nothing but press the boil and start buttons and hope it works as intended. It was a very nice gift to receive, but I have no way to discover specifically what each button would do. I could try them, but I am not sure I would be able to understand what the kettle is doing differently to the water, and I am enjoying my tea the way it is already. As far as I can tell, if I leave the water sitting in the kettle it eventually turns on and keeps it warm without me even doing anything, so I also have no idea what the keep warm button is actually meant to do. I have zero way to understand if the keep warm button is already on and it is a setting I turn on and off, or if I press the button when I need it to activate a specific function.
